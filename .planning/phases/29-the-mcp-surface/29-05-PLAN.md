  reading only the verdict is the name-based shortcut D-03 exists to prevent, one

  reading, and the departure is on three grounds, two of them locked decisions:
  (1) **D-05** locks `anno-` as the module prefix and names the disk-derived
  `readdirSync`-plus-stable-prefix-regex as the reason for it; (2) **D-13** locks
  the floor as *"re-expressed over the `anno-` prefix"*, and under bare names the
  renamed modules would not be counted by it at all, which is a lowering by
  construction; (3) **a guard research's ledger does not list** —
  `module-classification.test.ts`'s `inEnumerationOnDisk()` is itself a
  `startsWith("r2000-")` prefix enumeration, so a bare-name policy leaves the
  registry with no disk-derivable predicate at all. Under Policy A the measured
  count reaches 15 at this plan's close and 16 at the phase's close, so the literal
  reading is satisfiable and no interpretation is needed.
- **Planner-found guard, not in `29-RESEARCH.md`'s Guard Ledger (N-10):**
  `module-classification.test.ts`'s DIRECTION 6 asserts `disk.length > 0` with the
  message *"the in-scope enumeration is empty — the filter or the directory

### This plan owns the removal gate's rename discharge

Plan 29-02 built `scripts/check-no-regenerator2000.mjs` in wave 1 and, by its own
acceptance criterion, **the gate fails when an entry names a path that is not on
disk**. Its entries were authored against the pre-rename tree, because at wave 1

the wave that moves those paths. Two consequences, both of which this plan
discharges rather than inherits:

1. **Stale paths.** Every temporary-allow-list entry and every path-scoped

### The derived constraint, in both its halves

Plan 29-02's own acceptance criterion is that the gate fails when an entry names a
path that is not on disk. Two rules follow, and the **first half alone is not
sufficient** — an entry may cite a plan that owns the gate but cannot touch the
file the entry names, and that entry is then unreachable: its citing plan can
neither scrub the mention nor remove the entry, so it survives to the phase close
and makes 29-11's "the temporary block is empty" unreachable without either an
orphan or a permanent exemption for a statement that is false after the deletion.

1. **Reachability (allow-list entries).** A temporary allow-list entry may cite
   only a plan that carries **both** `scripts/check-no-regenerator2000.mjs`
   **and the file the entry names** in its own `files_modified`.
