## Decisions and readings adopted by this plan

- **D-03** (locked): the modules are renamed out from under the prefix **before**
  anything is deleted, and the set is driven by `module-classification.ts`, never
  by a prefix sweep.
- **D-16** (agent-resolved, taking the research recommendation): the rename set is
  **nine**, not eleven. `r2000-test-gate.ts` and `r2000-verify.ts` are deleted in
  plan 29-10, on the authority of their own registry `note` fields — *"Do NOT read
  this verdict as a claim that the module survives a prefix deletion"* and *"what a
  later phase inherits is the discipline … NOT the route"*. This is the registry
  working as designed: the verdict field and the note field are one record, and
  reading only the verdict is the name-based shortcut D-03 exists to prevent, one
  level up.
- **Naming policy adopted: every renamed survivor takes the `anno-` prefix
  (Policy A), and D-13's "raised not lowered" is read LITERALLY.** This departs
  from `29-RESEARCH.md`'s C-3 recommendation of bare names with a charitable
  reading, and the departure is on three grounds, two of them locked decisions:
  (1) **D-05** locks `anno-` as the module prefix and names the disk-derived
  `readdirSync`-plus-stable-prefix-regex as the reason for it; (2) **D-13** locks
  the floor as *"re-expressed over the `anno-` prefix"*, and under bare names the
  renamed modules would not be counted by it at all, which is a lowering by
  construction; (3) **a guard research's ledger does not list** —
  `module-classification.test.ts`'s `inEnumerationOnDisk()` is itself a
  `startsWith("r2000-")` prefix enumeration, so a bare-name policy leaves the
  registry with no disk-derivable predicate at all. Under Policy A the measured
  count reaches 15 at this plan's close and 16 at the phase's close, so the literal
  reading is satisfiable and no interpretation is needed.
- **Planner-found guard, not in `29-RESEARCH.md`'s Guard Ledger (N-10):**
  `module-classification.test.ts`'s DIRECTION 6 asserts `disk.length > 0` with the
  message *"the in-scope enumeration is empty — the filter or the directory
  resolution is broken"*. When this phase legitimately takes the enumeration to
  zero, that assertion fails on a correct tree. Its fate is decided here rather
  than discovered at the deletion: the registry gains a per-entry **fate** and a
  `discharged` scope, and DIRECTION 6's non-vacuity is re-expressed as a
  **discharge-closure relation** that stays checkable when the enumeration empties.
  The file keeps its name — it is not shipped in either tarball, so no name rule
  forces a rename, and keeping it is the cheaper and less disruptive fate.
- **D-12** (locked): `docs-r2000-decisions.test.ts` and `scripts/audit-gate.mjs`'s
  entry move together in one commit. Per `29-RESEARCH.md`'s open question 6 and the
  orchestrator's ruling, the test is **renamed and re-pointed, never deleted**,
  which keeps `DOCS_GUARD_FLOOR = 7` and `audit-integrity.test.ts` green with no
  floor change.
- **D-11** (locked): `r2000-answer-key.test.ts` is renamed and kept, never deleted.
  It reads `.planning/phases/11-*/evidence/` with no existence guard and is the
  second leg of the "do not archive phase directories" decision; deleting it would
  silently discharge that constraint.
- **C-4, adopted:** the renamed CLI is `anno-cli.ts`, invoked as `anno <verb>` —
  downstream of D-05's single-prefix rule. The invocation literal and the
  subcommand token are **not** changed here; they move in plan 29-09 together with
  the skill files that name them, because changing either half alone reds the
  FLOW-01 guard.
