- **D-01** (locked, user decision): the integration is deleted in this phase, not
  unregistered and not deferred. The owner's directive, recorded as data: the usage
  is decided to be removed, and if it cannot be removed right now everything about
  it must be disabled and never included in any tests. Presented with
  unregister-and-quarantine as the recommended option, the owner chose deletion.

  verdicts `glue` — while the CLI was renamed and kept.
- **D-16** (agent-resolved): two further modules are deleted rather than renamed,
  on the authority of their own registry `note` fields. **The consequence this plan
  must carry:** the verify module's two pinned false-pass transcripts are what the
  registry names as the thing that must survive. They ride forward as Phase 30
  fixtures; this plan moves them rather than letting them go with the module.
- **D-14's consequence, already recorded:** the two capability modules that
  implemented the symbol round trip and the enum generation survive under their new
  names with their **routes** removed and their **knowledge** kept. Their live
  round-trip test is deleted here, as an explicit choice, because its subject is
  withdrawn — and the withdrawal itself was already written into the project record
  by plan 29-07, so this deletion discharges a recorded decision rather than making
  a silent one.
- **Planner correction to `29-CONTEXT.md` (N-11), acted on here:** the proxy carries
  five references to the retired integration, not two. Plan 29-01 substituted the
  two that were line-count-neutral and plan 29-09 substituted the subcommand token
  in place; this plan removes the remaining two, which **does** change the line
  count above the cited lines. `CLAUDE.md`'s two `rewriteArguments()` citations are
  therefore corrected in this same commit — the instructions themselves say a
  mismatch is drift to re-verify, not evidence the constraint changed.
