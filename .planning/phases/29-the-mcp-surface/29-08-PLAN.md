every verb the manifest does not classify lives in a second committed register that
names why it exists.

property. The register is the other half — three verbs on the surface appear in no
manifest procedure, and criterion 5 positively requires one of them, so they need a
committed home that fails when a fourth appears without a named consumer.

check.

## Decisions and readings adopted by this plan

- **D-08** (locked): derivation is checked against the manifest, and verbs the
  manifest does not classify live in a second committed register, each entry citing
  a requirement id, so a verb added later with no named consumer **fails** rather
  than being reviewed. Amending the manifest was rejected: it describes upstream at
  a pinned commit and carries its own re-sync trigger and per-procedure hashes, so
  writing our verbs into it makes it describe us.
- **D-09** (locked): the cursor verb is folded into the disassemble verb's address
  argument, which is what its `adapt-to-address-input` disposition resolved to. The
  name mapping is therefore total over the manifest's verbs with exactly two
  documented departures.
- **C-2, adopted:** `anno_save_project`'s route reports the current revision and
  performs no write. The register records that choice against the verb, because "a
  route that reports rather than writes" is exactly what a later reader would
  mistake for an oversight.
- **F-5, adopted:** `anno_remove_scope` appears in no manifest procedure, so it
  goes in the register citing 28-REVIEW's finding as its named consumer — which is
  what the register is for.
- **C-5, adopted:** when the skill-coverage script's non-vacuity control needs a
  new subject, the subject is `anno_search` — it is `STORE-06`'s named requirement
  and it lives in this register, so if it silently leaves the surface both checks
  fire. That re-point lands with the skill files in plan 29-09; this plan only
  makes the register it depends on exist.

Plan 29-07 runs in this same wave. These are recorded here as decisions rather than
left to be discovered, which is the shape plan 29-05 already used for its own
same-wave floor coupling.

  plan's `files_modified`: plan 29-07 is in the same wave and already carries it,
  and this phase's zero-`files_modified`-overlap-within-a-wave guarantee outranks
  manifest tidiness. If a task here genuinely needs to change that count, it cannot
  fix the gate itself — say so and stop, rather than committing an edit that leaves
  the gate red for plan 29-09 to inherit two waves later and misattribute.

  a defect in this plan — re-run after 29-07's commit lands before reporting it.
  Record which of the two orderings actually occurred in the summary, so a reader
  is not left inferring it.
