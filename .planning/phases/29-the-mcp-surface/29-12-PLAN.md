D-17: rebuild the `render-memmap` verb onto the Phase 28 store.

`29-RESEARCH.md` measured `anno-memmap-render.ts` (then `r2000-memmap-render.ts`)
as having *"zero local imports"* at `:267` and `:569`, and `29-CONTEXT.md`'s
`<deferred>` block repeated it as *"no store call site"*. **All three measurements
were wrong**, and they were wrong for one mechanical reason: the file carries a
literal NUL byte at offset 12862 (line 291, the `"\0"` separator in the sidecar
hash canonicalisation), so GNU grep classifies it as binary and prints
`binary file matches` in place of the lines. Read with `grep -a`, the module
imports `runR2000Tool` at `:69`, uses it at `:106` inside `queryR2000Json()`, and
`renderMemoryMap()` calls that three times at `:353-355` — `r2000_get_blocks`,
`r2000_get_symbols` and `r2000_get_comments`. `checkRenderedMemoryMap()` (`:499`,
the `--check` path) reaches the same three calls through `renderMemoryMap()`, so
BOTH CLI entry points drive the child.

D-14 kept `render-memmap` on the strength of that falsified measurement — it was
supposed to be the one free verb. There was no free verb. Offered
rebuild-here / drop-to-Phase-30 / refuse-by-name, the owner chose **rebuild here**
(D-17).

Purpose: remove the last importer of the retired runner outside plan 29-10's own
deletion set, so that plan deletes `anno-tools.ts` rather than discovering it is
still load-bearing. The rebuild is pure reads and carries no ✓ Validated round
trip, which is why it is the cheapest of the four rebuilds and why it belongs in
this phase rather than following `export-lbl`/`import-lbl` into Phase 30.

Output: a `render-memmap` verb that renders from an anno store with no child
anywhere on its path, its three previously-gated tests converted to ungated
store-backed tests, and `RENDERER_VERSION` bumped to `"3"`.

**Why this plan is wave 6 and not folded into 29-07.** 29-07 is the natural home
by subject — it owns the two-verb CLI and the census re-point — but it is already
3 tasks at ~110k, and this rebuild is 3 tasks of its own; folding them makes a
6-task plan at ~215k, roughly twice the smart-zone budget. Placing it at wave 5
was the next option and it does not work either: the moment the renderer is
re-pointed, the three gated tests that populate a `.regen2000proj` and then call
`renderMemoryMap()` fail, so this plan must edit them, so it must move their
`regenerator2000` mention count, so it must re-pin that count in
`scripts/check-no-regenerator2000.mjs` — which plan 29-09 already owns at wave 5.
That is a same-wave `files_modified` collision, so this plan takes wave 6 and
plans 29-10 and 29-11 shift to waves 7 and 8. The wave count goes 7 → 8. That is
the correct trade: the ordering guarantee (the gate proven at wave 1 before the

importer is gone rather than beside it.
