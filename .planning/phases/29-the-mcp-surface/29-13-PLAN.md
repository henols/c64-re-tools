<objective>
Close verification gaps 1 and 4 — the two MCP-04 defects that live in
`anno-tools.ts` and that between them falsify the phase goal's own words,
"shaped for an agent rather than a cursor".

**Gap 1 (CR-01).** `anno_disassemble` answers a wholly-out-of-image address with
`isError:false`, `instructions:0` and an `end_address` numerically BELOW the
`address` asked about. Its sibling `anno_read_region` refuses the identical
input correctly with `{available:false, reason}`. Two causes, both here:
`sliceSpan()` guards only the low and high bounds and never an inverted span, and
`dispatchDisassemble()` narrows the requested end to the image's last address
BEFORE slicing, so an out-of-image start produces an empty slice instead of the
`null` that reaches `outsideImage()`.

**Gap 4 (CR-06).** A nested `anno_batch_execute` relying on the documented store
inheritance is refused WHOLE, every time. Phase-one validation recurses with the
child's RAW arguments while phase-two execution recurses with the child's
EFFECTIVE arguments, so the two phases disagree about what the inner payload is.
The refusal message says there is no ambient store to inherit while the tool's
own description says the store is named once at the top level and every inner
call inherits it. `ANNO_MAX_BATCH_DEPTH` therefore governs a shape unreachable at
depth 1 by the documented route — the cap has no reachable positive control.

**Folded in (stated as an inclusion decision, not silently absorbed): WR-10**,
because it is the same file, one function, and it sits on the one verb whose
output is used as a compare-and-swap guard.

**Explicitly NOT folded in**, and each is left for a later pass rather than
grown into this one: WR-01, WR-02, WR-04, WR-06, WR-11, WR-12 (all `anno-*`
warnings that need their own design decision), WR-03 and WR-05 (different files,
handled nowhere in this gap round — recorded below in `<verification>`), and
WR-09 (deferred to Phase 30 by the verification itself).

Purpose: MCP-04 is recorded `Gaps Found`; these are the two reproduced defects

Output: two behavioural repairs in the production surface, and three tests that
pin properties nobody asserted — the two read verbs' AGREEMENT, the batch cap's
