instruction `routine-queue-walker` has, and the delivery path for one of
REPOINT-01's five absorbed procedures — prints a full report of zeros and exits

so this one needs a production change.

`check-skill-tool-coverage.mjs` resolves tool and verb NAMES and never an
invocation's arguments. A name-only floor cannot see a dead command. That gate is
correct at what it does; what is missing is a sibling that checks the arguments.

**WR-07** — `prg-image.ts`'s stated reachability reason names an import
`anno-cli.ts` does not have, and this plan changes that file's imports again, so
leaving it would compound a false rationale. **WR-14, in part** — the mechanical
rename left user-facing strings and cross-references on the retired vocabulary,
including the message a user gets for an unknown verb. Three of its five sites
are taken here; the two internal identifier renames are dropped from this round
on a recorded decision. See `<wr14_scope_decision>` below.

`exemptionFor()` short-circuit). It is a real latent defect, it is correct today
only by array order, and it is in the phase's own removal gate — but that gate is
green tree-wide with an empty allow-list and touching its exemption walk in a
gap-closure round risks the one mechanism every other plan here is verified
against. It stays a recorded warning. Also not folded in: WR-01, WR-02, WR-03,
WR-04, WR-05, WR-06, WR-11, WR-12 (no gap plan opens their files), and WR-09
(deferred to Phase 30 by the verification itself).
