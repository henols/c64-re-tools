<wr02_resolution>
**`29-VERIFICATION.md` requires this contradiction resolved one way or the other,
forbids silently widening it into a full test rewrite, and requires the choice to
be named with its reason. The choice is made here.**

**Chosen: correct the three headers to the property the test checks, AND add the
one cheap direction that closes half the gap — the positional half. Do not
attempt argument-to-call-site association.**

**Why not the full strengthening.** The property the headers claim — *this
argument reached that filesystem call without passing through the seam* — needs
per-argument dataflow through a 900-plus-line CLI. `seamCallCount()` cannot get
there, and neither can tightening `>=` to `==`: five arguments confined with one
confined twice still counts six. That is a static-analysis project, not a gap fix,
and widening into it is what this round is fenced against.

**Why not headers-only either.** The most-cited half of the claim is genuinely
achievable at low cost. The test already derives the **flag** half of
`CLI_PATH_ARGUMENTS` from `VERB_OPTIONS` (direction 2), so a new path-shaped flag
joins the audit automatically. Only the **positional** half sits outside, and
`anno-cli.test.ts:658-671` already demonstrates parsing a verb's USAGE synopsis
line for its declared arguments — the analog is a reuse, not an invention. This
plan owns both files, so it costs one direction in one commit.

**What the headers will say afterwards**, and this is the part that discharges the
prohibition: they state that the test enumerates every caller-supplied path
argument — flags derived from `VERB_OPTIONS`, positionals derived from the USAGE
synopsis — and fails when the inventory and the surface disagree **or** when the
count of confinement call sites falls below the inventory's size; and they state
in terms what it does **not** do: it does not associate a particular argument with
a particular call site, so six arguments confined once and five confined with one
confined twice are indistinguishable to it. Naming the residual is what keeps the
header from becoming a fresh warrant not to check.

**Reversibility:** `reversible` — comment text and one added test direction.
</wr02_resolution>
