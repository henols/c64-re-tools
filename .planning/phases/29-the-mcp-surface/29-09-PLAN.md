## Decisions and readings adopted by this plan

- **Corrected counts, measured 2026-08-29 and re-measured at execution:** the
  skill tree has **5** files naming **17** distinct old-family tool names, and
  **10** files mentioning the old subject at all. `29-CONTEXT.md`'s "9 skill files
  naming 18 tools" and `REPOINT-01`/ROADMAP Phase 31's "18 distinct tool names" are
  superseded. **Re-measure at execution time and use the measured figure** — the
  research documents disagree because they grepped different things.
- **D-10 / CUT-05, resolved in one change that names which side is correct.** The
  fork-honesty guard asserts a skill still contains a literal naming the export
  route, so cleansing the skills fails its assertion while keeping the string fires
  the removal gate. **The skill is the side that moves.** Its live route is removed
  and replaced by a dated withdrawal notice that names the route under its future
  name; the assertion is re-pointed at that notice's literal, which stays true when
  Phase 30 restores the route under that name. The guard therefore stays
  non-vacuous — deleting the notice fails it — and the skill stops advertising a
  route that does not exist.
- **`packer-finding.mjs`'s recorded provenance value is decided, not
  string-replaced.** ROADMAP Phase 31 requires exactly that. The value is a stored
  fact about a past run, not a route: renaming it to a current verb would claim a
  run that never happened, and keeping a tool-name-shaped literal leaves the skill
  tree carrying an old-family name forever. The decision is to **keep the fact and
  retire the literal** — the provenance is restated as prose naming the retired
  analyser and the verb class it used, with a date, and no tool-name-shaped token.
- **C-5, adopted:** the skill-coverage script's non-vacuity control needs a new
  subject because its current one names a verb of the retired family. The new
  subject is the search verb — it is `STORE-06`'s named requirement and it is the
  register's own headline entry, so a verb silently leaving the surface fires this
  control and the register guard together rather than neither.
