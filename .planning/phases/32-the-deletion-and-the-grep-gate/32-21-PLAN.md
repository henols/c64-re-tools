**The defect, re-derived by the planner rather than quoted.** The plant post-condition computes
the introduction of the recorded replacement as a whole-file difference — the count after the
mutation minus the count before it. That subtraction cannot see a replacement that textually
OVERLAPS its own pre-existing occurrence. Exactly one of the 35 committed descriptors has that
shape: `src/mcp/vice/hop-chain-comments.test.ts`, which plants into
`src/mcp/vice/absorbed-answer-key.test.ts` with a recorded replacement equal to its recorded find
with one newline prepended. Measured at HEAD `811b516`: find occurs 1 time, the replacement
occurs 1 time before AND 1 time after, so the difference is 0 and the post-condition throws —
while the mutation is REAL and the descriptor is HONEST, the file changing by exactly +1 byte.

**And it is not cosmetic.** A refused plant sets the row's failed flag, which sets the run's
hard-failure flag, which suppresses the registry write-back. So while this one row stands
refused, an `--all` sweep can NEVER write the registry: the verifier's run printed
`registry: NOT written (a row's plant was refused; a row was unmeasurable)`. The artefact at the
centre of `CUT-04` cannot be regenerated by its own whole-set command.

**Where the defect came from, so the record is straight.** The sentence in the code comment
asserting that the narrowing is free was adopted verbatim from round 2's own gap text, which
promised the overlap case was still caught by that form. It is not caught — it is *refused*. The
defect is inherited from the gap text, not invented by the executor, and the executor found it,
recorded it in the file's own refusal comment, and filed it as an open `unmet-truth` (ledger
entry 35, 07:18Z) before any review existed. This plan says so rather than quietly correcting a
sentence.

**What is NOT wrong, and is not re-opened.** No false pass is possible; no committed record is
corrupted (35/35 recorded-red objects structurally intact, 35 distinct excerpts, 35/35 green
controls); 34 of the 35 rows re-measure. The one that came back UNMEASURABLE in the verifier's

`audit-gate: OK -- 9 docs guards green, 7 milestone audits scanned, 5 declaring a gated status`.

**Why the standing guard is the point, not the re-run.** `WR-36` records that three of this
instrument's behaviours — the post-condition arithmetic, the SKIPPED reporting and the
refused-plant reporting — ship with no standing coverage at all, proven only by one-off manual
runs. In a phase whose criterion is *a guard that cannot be made to fail has not been
re-pointed*, that is the strongest argument for this plan adding a contract test for the
arithmetic it changes rather than proving it once by hand. Rounds 1 and 2 each closed their gaps
AND left a regression in this same file; every fix here lands with a guard that is watched
failing first.

Two cheap hardenings land in the SAME FUNCTION gap 1 edits and are therefore taken rather than
