<objective>
GAP CLOSURE, round 1. **Gap 3 — evidence hygiene in the instrument whose whole product is
evidence.**

Two defects in `scripts/audit-mutation-harness.mjs`, bundled by the verifier into one fix
because they are the same class: the instrument recording something other than what
happened.

**CR-02.** `plant()` counts occurrences with `text.split(find)`, which is literal, but
writes with `text.replace(find, replace)`, which is not — `$$`, `$&`, backtick-`$`, `$'`,
`$n` and `$<name>` are still interpreted in the replacement string. Row
`src/mcp/vice/r2000-enum-gen.test.ts` records a replacement containing a doubled dollar
sign; what reached disk had the pair collapsed and the hex sigil deleted. The row's own
captured excerpt proves it — the failing comparison shows a five-character value with no
sigil where the recorded mutation would have produced a six-character one with it.

**Scope limit, measured by the verifier rather than inferred, and this plan does not
re-litigate it.** The row's CONCLUSION survives: the verifier applied the literally-recorded
mutation with a replacer function and the guard still exited 1 on the same named assertion,
and re-running the real harness reproduced the committed excerpt byte-for-byte. Only 1 of 61
rows is affected. The two other rows carrying a dollar sign are safe by luck, not by design.
So this is a correction to a record, not a reversal of a verdict — and the plan must not
write it as though a verdict changed.

**CR-04.** `runGuard()` maps `result.status ?? 1`, so a child killed by a signal — status
`null`, signal non-null — becomes a recorded exit status of 1 with a passing pre-plant
control. The verifier audited all 35 recorded reds and none is a signal artifact, so this is
a latent defect in the instrument, not corrupted data. It is nonetheless a live path: this
host runs earlyoom and `node --test` suites are memory-hungry, and it would manufacture
exactly the false red this phase exists to prevent.

Implements **D-03** (a fate row records four fields, and the plant descriptor is one of
them), **D-04** (a committed, re-runnable harness produces the evidence rather than a
hand-worked transcript — the property CR-02 broke), **D-05** (the verdict decides what
evidence the row owes), **D-08** (only text that misleads is corrected; everything
historical stays byte-identical), **D-15** (the evidence artifact carries raw commands, raw
output, broker state and the commit measured) and **D-17** (the harness is a committed phase
instrument, not a CI job — so fixing it changes no CI surface).

Purpose: the verifier's words — *"reproducibility of recorded evidence is the property this
phase exists to establish."* An artifact that promises a reader a hand-reproducible
find-and-replace and does not deliver one is a defect in the phase's own product.

Output: a harness that writes what it records and cannot mistake a kill for a failure; one
row re-measured; one authorised line correction with a dated explanation; and one new
evidence file.
