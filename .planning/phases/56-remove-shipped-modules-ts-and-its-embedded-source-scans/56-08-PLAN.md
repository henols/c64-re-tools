<cut_rule>
## What qualifies for removal

IN SCOPE: a case whose SUBJECT is the source TEXT of a module. The route does not matter. It may
be the doomed scanning helpers, a raw `readFileSync` of a sibling `.ts` or `.mts`, a module-scope
path constant read at call time, or a locally written comment stripper.

NOT IN SCOPE: a case that reads a non-source artifact, such as `package.json` parsed as JSON, a
JSON data file, an assembled fixture, or a directory listing.

- **D-01.** Whole-case removal is the default.
- **D-02.** A case is exempt only when removal of every source-text assertion still leaves at
  least one assertion that exercises production behaviour. A setup-only remainder goes whole. A
  remainder whose assertions all derive from scanned text goes whole.
- **D-03.** An exempt case is stripped in place, byte-for-byte, and renamed to what it now proves.
- **D-04 (prohibition).** Never remove a case and re-add its behavioural half as a new case.
- **Helper rule.** A module-scope helper, constant or import goes with its cases only when every
  caller is itself being removed. grep the whole file for other callers first.
- **D-10 (prohibition).** Write NO new prose anywhere. The SUMMARY is the one exception.
- **Stale comments in test files stay stale on purpose.** Leave surviving comment prose
  byte-identical, following D-13's reasoning.
- **Stop and report. Never adjust.** If a cut trips another file's pinned assertion, that
  measurement IS the finding.
- **D-17.** Compare SETS, never counts. Never pin a case count in an assertion.
</cut_rule>
