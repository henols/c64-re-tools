<objective>
Split `vice.ts` into a shared module and the fork transport, before anything is deleted from it.

`vice.ts` is 772 lines and is two modules wearing one file. The ROADMAP Notes said "ten non-test modules import from it, most for the shared error hierarchy"; a fresh grep finds **23** non-test importers and **18** test importers. Worse, the Notes imply `activeInstance()`/`useInstance()`/`mcpHost()` are fork-only. They are not: `buildHeldLease()` at `vice-proxy.ts:2349` reads `activeInstance()` on every tool call through `ensureBrokerLease()`, on the stock path. Deleting `vice.ts` wholesale would break stock lease acquisition, not just fork transport. This is the single highest-risk misread in the phase.

Purpose: make every later deletion a deletion of genuinely fork-only code, with a typecheck that proves it.
Output: `vice-errors.ts` (new), all importers repointed, `vice-sync.ts` (dead code) gone.
</objective>
