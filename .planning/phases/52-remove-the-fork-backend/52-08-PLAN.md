<objective>
Rewrite every fork-routing site in shipped skill text as a stated permanent limitation, and clear the wider two-backend framing those sites sit inside.

This is the half of the phase that does not get simpler. The project's own `SKILL-01` constraint says a skill written against the full fork surface *breaks* on stock rather than degrading, which is why playbooks must name either the stock route or the fork requirement. With no fork, every "requires the fork" route becomes a dead end — and deleting it would leave a reader with a silently-missing capability, which is exactly the failure mode the constraint exists to prevent.

Research found **9** routing sites (the hand-off list named 8; `vice-wedge-triage/SKILL.md:132` was missed). A fresh census finds **27** fork-mentioning lines across **9** skill files, so the routing sites are a subset of a wider two-backend framing — `vice-wedge-triage/SKILL.md` alone carries 12 lines, most of them prose like "on the fork, four states look identical" that is not a route but is still a claim about a backend that no longer exists.

Purpose: land criterion 3's shipped-prose half.
Output: nine skill files free of fork framing, each former route now a stated permanent limitation; plus the two ride-along defects fixed in the same pass.
</objective>
