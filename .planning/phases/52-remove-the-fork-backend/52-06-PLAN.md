<objective>
Collapse backend detection to nothing, on both sides of the container boundary, and remove every fork-conditional test branch.

Two processes resolve the backend independently today — the MCP server (`vice-proxy.ts:337`, `const ACTIVE_BACKEND = backendDetect.resolvedBackend()`) and the host broker (`vice-broker.mts:69`) — and cross-check each other's verdicts over the control plane. With one backend the comparison is a tautology.

The test half is larger than research measured. A fresh census finds **19** test files carrying `"fork"` or `VICE_BACKEND` — 13 in the automated gate (`backend-detect` 22 hits, `broker-control` 10, `broker-launch` 8, `stock-dispatch` 6, `capability-registry` 6, `text-capability-probe` 5, `text-tools` 3, `vice-broker-client` 2, `host-tool` 2, `host-tool-oracle` 5 `VICE_BACKEND` hits, `host-tool-transport` 1, `vice-broker-acquire` 1, `vice-broker-supervision` 1) and 6 dispositioned manual-only (`vice-proxy`, `stock-a4-checkpoint-flood`, `stock-broker-live`, `stock-live-broker-monitor`, `text-monitor-live`, and `fork-live` which plan 52-05 already deleted). This plan owns the broker/detect cluster; `stock-dispatch.test.ts`, `capability-registry.test.ts`, `text-capability-probe.test.ts` and `text-tools.test.ts` belong to plan 52-07 because their fork references are registry-shaped, not detection-shaped.

Purpose: land criterion 1's detection half and the bulk of criterion 7.
Output: no `VICE_BACKEND`, no `probeBackend()`, no cross-check, regenerated `resources/*.mjs`, and fork branches structurally removed from 15 test files.
</objective>
