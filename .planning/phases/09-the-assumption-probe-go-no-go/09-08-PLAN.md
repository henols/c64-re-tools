<objective>
Make the verdict **discoverable**. Criterion 5 is satisfied by plan 09-07's machine-readable
frontmatter key, but only for a reader who already knows the filename. This plan closes that
gap: a session that opens `STATE.md` or the ROADMAP finds the verdict and the path to the
evidence without being told where to look.

Purpose: the ROADMAP's own instruction is "read it before planning Phase 10", and `R2000-16`'s
wording is "before any further plan is written". A pointer that only exists inside the artifact
it points at does not enforce that.

**Why this is a separate plan, and why it must not run inside a worktree.** Its deliverable
*is* the content of `.planning/STATE.md` and `.planning/ROADMAP.md`. Worktree isolation forbids
executors from touching those two files — the orchestrator owns those writes — and
`execute-plan` strips them from the commit automatically. That is correct for ordinary tracking
updates and **wrong** when the edit is the point: the plan would report success and deliver
nothing. Dispatch it with `USE_WORKTREES_FOR_PLAN=false`, sequentially on the main tree, and
let it own its own tracking writes. Plan 09-07 deliberately kept its hands off both files for
this reason.

Output: a STATE.md decision entry and ROADMAP updates, both pointing at
`docs/phase9-regenerator2000-probe-findings.md` as the single source of truth.
</objective>
