## The real refusal, already reproduced at plan time

Reproduced here so the executor confirms a known result rather than hunting for one. Run
against the pinned dxa 0.1.5 binary on 2026-09-04, on a 65,536-byte flat image:

- The command completed in **21 ms** and exited **0**, with empty stderr.
- It produced a **699,116-byte, 21,848-line** listing.
- Its final line reads `ffff eb 48 7d 	.byt $eb` — a THREE-byte hex column at `$ffff`
  whose directive emits ONE byte. The trailing `48 7d` are the image's own first two bytes:
  dxa's dump column wraps past top-of-memory and re-prints them. The listing also ends
  without a trailing newline.
- Feeding that listing to the Phase 23 evidence parser with a declared size of 65536 throws
  `dxa-listing-parse: accounted byte total 65538 does not equal expected image size 65536.`

That is a real, unplanned, non-hand-planted refusal from a real run — exactly what `DXA-02`
criterion 2 asks for, and it is the reason plan 35-01's decision A-04 replaced the running
emission count with distinct in-window coverage.
