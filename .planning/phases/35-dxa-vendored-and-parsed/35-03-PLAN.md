## Planner decisions recorded here

**A-07 — two tiers, both printed, neither chosen after the fact.** `PARTITION_SOURCE_DERIVED`
for inputs where an ACME source and its `-r` report exist; `PARTITION_BYTE_DERIVED` for
real-release-class inputs with no source. Both tiers are always named in the output, and a
run states which tier produced its numbers. Reasoning: choosing a tier after seeing which
flatters the result is precisely the failure `DXA-04` exists to prevent, and printing both
tier names — one with numbers, one with a stated reason for being inapplicable — makes the
choice visible in the artifact rather than in a person's memory. Reversibility:
**reversible**.

**A-08 — the positive class is `data`.** Continuity with Phase 23's own framing, so Phase
38's comparisons stay apples-to-apples in vocabulary even where the derivation method
differs. Stated on every rate line rather than assumed. Reversibility: **reversible** — it
is a reporting convention, and either choice is internally consistent as long as it is
stated.

**A-09 — the byte-derived tier's certain set is exactly two facts, and its denominator is
what it can prove.** The `.prg` header is definitionally not part of the loaded image, so
it is excluded from the partition entirely rather than classified. A BASIC tokenised stub
at `$0801` is a fully documented, source-independent byte grammar — link-address word, line
number word, tokens, `$00` line terminator, `$00 $00` program-end marker — and bytes covered
by a successfully-parsed stub are never 6502 instructions by construction of how the BASIC
interpreter consumes them. Nothing else about a real release is decidable from bytes alone.
A screen matrix conventionally sits at `$0400` and a charset at `$1000`, but that convention
is not universal and asserting it without source or execution would be exactly the silent
guess this requirement exists to prevent. Reversibility: **reversible** — a later phase with
a non-execution-derived source of graphics ranges may widen the certain set; widening a
partition is additive and invalidates no earlier rate as long as the denominator is
restated.

**A-10 — this phase computes NO rate about dxa on real cracked code.** The ROADMAP is
explicit: real-release rate numbers belong to Phase 38, and no plan in this phase may
quietly claim `PROOF-01`. What this phase owes Phase 38 is the partition script and the
discipline that every rate carries its denominator and its positive class. The fixture-tier
numbers reproduced here are a REPRODUCTION of Phase 23's already-published fixture
measurement, not a new claim about dxa. Reversibility: **one-way in reputation** — a rate
once published cannot be unpublished, which is why the constraint is a prohibition rather
than a preference.
