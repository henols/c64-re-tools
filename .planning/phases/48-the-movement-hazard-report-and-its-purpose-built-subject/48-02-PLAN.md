<objective>
Grow the synthetic subject from one hazard class to three, by planting a non-canonical
indexed jump table and a real VIC-II alignment dependency, each documented against the
published idiom it deviates from, and each committed with the control that shows the
detector could decline.

Purpose: criterion 3's bar is that each class is observed firing on a NON-CANONICAL planted
variant and observed NOT firing on a negative control. That bar is only meaningful if the
planted variants are chosen from a taxonomy written before the detectors, against the
textbook idiom, rather than reverse-engineered from what a detector happens to match. This
plan writes the subject halves for class 1 and class 3 — including, per D-48-A, the VIC-II
hardware reading of page-alignment dependence, which composes with the sprite, charset,
level and music tables the subject must carry anyway and fails VISIBLY with no error signal
when deliberately mis-aligned.
Output: two new subject source files, a deliberately mis-aligned twin image, and a fixture
test that asserts the planted constructions at the byte level and re-derives both images
from source on every run.
</objective>
