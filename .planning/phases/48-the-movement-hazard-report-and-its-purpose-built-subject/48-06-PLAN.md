<objective>
Write the document that makes the subject reviewable, and prove the subject survives the
whole rebuild path: exported into a tree of real files, reassembled by a real assembler, and
byte-compared.

Purpose: criterion 1 asks for two things the rest of the phase cannot supply. First, a
document stating per class which variant was chosen and why it is not the textbook idiom, and
showing the four classes are structurally different rather than one shape reskinned — that
can only be written once all four exist, and it is the artifact a reviewer uses to judge
whether the fixture was written honestly. Second, the subject must actually go through the
multi-file export and reassemble, which is the named cost of shipping the fixture and the
detector together: the rebuild path was proven on other fixtures and has never seen this one.
Output: the fixture design document, a reassembly test that runs the real rebuild path on the
subject, and the recorded on-screen observations for both images.
</objective>
