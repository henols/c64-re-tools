<objective>
Finish the synthetic subject: plant the fourth hazard class as its non-canonical timer-based
variant, plant the self-modification the detector is designed to MISS, and commit the
annotation-store export that describes the whole program.

Purpose: the fourth class is the one with no static algorithm at all, and the second
self-modification is the one with no static operand to match. Both are planted deliberately
so the report's honesty structure has something real to be honest about — a flag the detector
can raise but never verify, and a hazard it cannot see. A phase that shipped only the hazards
its detectors find would have proven nothing about what happens at the edge of what they can
do. The committed store export then turns the subject from a pile of bytes into the input
the reassembly criterion runs on.
Output: the raster source part, the second self-modification, the regenerated images, and a
committed store export describing every byte of the subject.
</objective>
