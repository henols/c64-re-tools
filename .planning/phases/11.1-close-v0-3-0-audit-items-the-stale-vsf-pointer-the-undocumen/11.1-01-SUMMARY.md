## Accomplishments

- `r2000-cli.ts`'s USAGE text and its live `bootstrap` `.vsf` refusal no longer say "Phase 11's job, not this CLI's" — both now name `.planning/todos/pending/2026-08-20-vsf-as-a-bootstrap-input.md` and keep the machine-type-coincidence WHY.
