<plan_decision_D-05-05>
**Success criterion 5 is verified by a committed script (research option a), not by a
manual pass.**

The research left this as a planner judgment call between building
`scripts/check-skill-tool-coverage.mjs` now and recording a one-off manual pass. Building
it wins on three counts: criterion 5 is a **success criterion**, so it needs something that
can be re-run rather than a claim made once; Phase 8's `DIST-01` requires a support table
**derived from the shipped manifests rather than maintained by hand**, which is the same
extraction plus a rendering step; and a manual pass cannot notice the day a later phase
makes one of the allowlisted tools work.

The script's design point is that its allowlist **shrinks by failing**: every tool listed
as "pending a later phase" is asserted to be **absent** from the stock manifest, so when
Phase 7 lands `vice_cycles_stopwatch` the script fails until the entry is removed. An
allowlist that only ever grows is how a coverage check rots.
</plan_decision_D-05-05>

<plan_decision_D-05-08>
**Criterion 5's exception list is three tools, not two — `vice_keyboard_restore` is the
third, and the discrepancy is recorded rather than silently resolved.**

The ROADMAP's criterion 5 says "except for the two tools proven unrecoverable
(`vice_sid_get_state`, `vice_keyboard_matrix`)". Extracting every `vice_*` reference from
the six skills finds a **third**: `vice_keyboard_restore`, called by
`c64-program-recon/references/control-flow.md`, present on the fork manifest, absent from
the stock manifest, and **already dispositioned as a hard loss** by
`docs/stock-vice-parity.md` §A item 2 — the low-level keyboard family
(`matrix`, `chord`, `key_press/release`, `restore`), all of which fail for the same reason:
`KEYBOARD_FEED` (0x72) only injects PETSCII text into the buffer and cannot pulse the
RESTORE/NMI line. It is covered by no requirement and appears in no phase's scope.

**Decision: treat it exactly like the other two** — allowlisted with a reason and the same
Phase 8 route (`BACK-05` for the runtime error, `SKILL-01` for the playbook note),
recorded in `docs/stock-vice-parity.md` as a correction to criterion 5's count, and given a
fork-requirement note in `control-flow.md` in this plan. **Do not amend the ROADMAP's
criterion text** — that is the developer's call, and the summary must surface it as an open
item for Phase 8 planning rather than quietly rewriting a success criterion.
</plan_decision_D-05-08>
