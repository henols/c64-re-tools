<objective>
Make the drift guard trustworthy: watch it fail on a planted divergence before
trusting it, and prove by behaviour — not by inspection — that it compares facts
rather than bytes.

Plan 61-01 built the guard and showed it green. A permanently-green test is not
evidence (`ENGINEERING_RULES.md` §6). This plan adds the failure direction
(`GEN-03`) and the tolerance direction (roadmap criterion 4), plus the parser
fidelity check that keeps the two sides from sharing a bug.

Purpose: `GEN-03` in full, and the half of `GEN-02` that the roadmap states as a
negative — divergence is caught by comparing parsed records, and a change that
alters no fact leaves the guard green.

Output: additional cases in `src/mcp/vice/prereq-readme-gen.test.ts`.
</objective>

Tolerance cases. Using the scratch-pair helper from Task 1, mutate the scratch README's generated regions in ways that alter no fact, then assert `auditGeneratedReadme` returns an empty array for each:

- widen every cell's padding inside both regions;
- reverse the data-row order inside the overview region, and independently inside the ecosystem region;

A header that no longer matches the module's exported column constants means the parse is reading different columns, which is a real divergence and must not be swallowed by the same leniency that forgives padding. State that distinction in a comment above the case — it is the line between "reflow" and "different table", and a later reader will otherwise be tempted to widen the tolerance until the guard tolerates the divergence too.

Round-trip fidelity cases. Over the REAL declaration, for each region: derive the rows with the generator's exported derive function, render them with the generator's exported render function, parse that rendering with `parseGeneratedRegion`, map the parsed cells back into the row shape, and `assert.deepEqual` against the derived rows. ## Flagged Assumptions

1. **`GEN-03`'s edge class is unresolved (spec-less probe, `unclassified`).** The
   deterministic edge probe could not classify `GEN-03` into a boundary, precision,
   concurrency or failure-mode category, and it is NOT auto-resolved with a
   backstop. This plan answers it with five planted divergences chosen to span both
   regions and both failure shapes (a changed fact, and a structurally broken
   region). Whether five is the right set — and in particular whether a divergence
   in a `platforms` cell rather than a `text` cell deserves its own case — is a
   judgement a reviewer should confirm before the phase seals.
2. **The tolerance list is taken literally from roadmap criterion 4** — reflowed
   whitespace, different column padding, different row order. Anything else a
   future editor might consider harmless (a changed column header, a renamed
   region marker, a reordered column) is deliberately NOT tolerated, and the
   column-reorder counter-case is there to stop the tolerance being widened by
   someone who reads criterion 4 as a general licence.
