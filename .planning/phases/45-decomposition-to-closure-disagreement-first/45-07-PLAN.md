<objective>
Derive and execute the three Ghidra fixtures — the hard ones — and commit
their stores in the D-02 export form.

These three are where this phase's two named pitfalls live. `bank.prg` and
`bank-path-dependent.prg` are the bank-state fixtures: one straight-line, one
built precisely to contain an address whose target depends on which caller got
there first. `charset-phantom.prg` is 4097 bytes, is the only fixture in the
tree that writes `$D011` and `$D018`, and carries a 2048-byte region that is
simultaneously valid character-set data and a real chain of callable
`jsr`/`rts` subroutines.

A correction this plan carries deliberately: 45-CONTEXT.md flag 4 anticipated
"typing a 4K table". RESEARCH.md §9 traced the fixture's own source by hand and
found that its `start` really calls into that chain and really unwinds back
through it — so once it is executed, essentially all 2044 bytes are OBSERVED
EXECUTING, and DECOMP-01's own soundness rule ("a range observed executing IS
code") forces the region to be typed `code`. That is the fixture doing exactly
what its header comment says a phantom routine is. The plan states the
correction rather than adopting it silently.

Purpose: criterion 4's evidence comes from here, and criterion 5's only
possible home is here.

Output: three committed `.annostore.json` artifacts, the Ghidra README's
provenance entries, and an evidence document recording every route, verdict
and decline.
</objective>
