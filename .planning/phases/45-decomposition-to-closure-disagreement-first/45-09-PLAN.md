<objective>
Walk the three Ghidra fixtures to closure — and write criterion 4's canonical
decline while doing it.

`bank-path-dependent.prg` exists precisely because it contains an address
whose target depends on which caller reached it: `$D000,x` is character ROM
under `$01 = $33` and RAM under `$01 = $34`, and the fixture calls `probe`
twice under both. Pitfall 5 (bank-state collapse) and Pitfall 12 (fabricated
indirect targets) both land here, and both are prevented by the same
discipline — decline with a reason. This plan is where that discipline stops
being a rule and becomes a committed comment.

`charset-phantom.prg` needs its dual use written down: plan 45-07 typed its
charset region `code` because it was observed executing, and the fact that the
VIC-II reads the identical bytes as a character set belongs in a comment, not
in a data type that would contradict the observation.

Purpose: a confident wrong symbol is worse than an absent one, and this is the
fixture family where a confident wrong symbol is easiest to produce.

Output: three closed stores, installed enums including `$D011` and `$D018`,
and an evidence document naming every decline with both bank states.
</objective>
