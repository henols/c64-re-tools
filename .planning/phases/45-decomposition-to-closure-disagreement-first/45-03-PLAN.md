<objective>
Give criterion 5 its decoder and its route.

Two things land here, both inside the module that already owns register
decoding — `anno-enum-gen.ts`. First, the ONE owning decoder D-16 requires:
`decomposeRegisterValue()`, which splits a written byte into one named term
per bit-field, arithmetically exact. Second, the fetch-and-install route D-15
asks to be rebuilt around the heuristics that deliberately survived plan 29-10
(`variantNameFor()`, `pairSearchRows()`, `planEnumsForPairing()`,
`sanitizeVariantMap()`, `buildEnumGenerationReport()` — all live, all tested,
none of them touched here).

The insight that keeps this to one decoder instead of two: `variantNameFor()`
already decodes a value into per-field tokens and joins them with `_`. The
OR-ed decomposition D-17 wants is that same token list, unjoined, each term
carrying its own masked value. `lda #D011_YSCROLL3 | D011_ROW25 |
D011_SCREENON | D011_TEXT` and the whole-value member
`lda #D011_YSCROLL3_ROW25_SCREENON_TEXT` are then the same vocabulary split at
the same boundaries — which is what makes the byte-diff oracle a proof of the
decomposition rather than of a coincidence.

Purpose: `ANNO-13` has had no route since 2026-08-30 and no phase owned its
return. D-15 makes this phase own the enum half.

Output: the decoder, the rebuilt route, their tests, and the corrected
`ANNO-13` withdrawal notice.

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
