<objective>
Correct one clause and its propagation. Phase 17's records assert that a human, having
comprehended the full six-for/five-against evidence at plan 17-02 task 1's
`gate="blocking-human"` checkpoint, declined to select between `restate` and `keep-dated`
and delegated the call to the orchestrating session.

The attendance and the delegation are REAL and stay stated. The primary session transcript
establishes them: the checkpoint prompt was rendered at 2026-08-23T08:32:48Z, a human
answered 298 seconds later at 08:37:46Z with the free text `you decide` (not either option
label), and the orchestrator then selected `keep-dated`. Auto-mode is ruled out — it
resolves to the first option label instantly, with no latency and no custom string.

What is NOT evidenced, and cannot be by any artifact, is COMPREHENSION. The orchestrator
synthesized that clause into its own continuation hand-off, under a header reading "record
this accurately, do not embellish it", then mandated it for publication — and it propagated
through six documents and three re-transcriptions unchallenged, because the anti-overstatement
guard it wrote for itself policed only the selection-versus-delegation axis and never noticed
it was crossing the attendance-versus-comprehension axis.

A second, subtler over-claim rides along: "shown the full evidence (six for, five against)"
is true of the terminal render that preceded the decision prompt — as compressed paraphrases
of `17-RESEARCH.md`'s eleven items — but overstated as to the prompt itself, which carried
only the two option labels plus one strongest-argument line each.

Purpose: this is the audit-integrity phase. Its own stated purpose was to avoid "a record
that reads as more human-decided than it was". An unfalsifiable comprehension claim is
precisely that failure mode, and the fix is not to erase the human but to state exactly what
is evidenced — no more and no less. Overcorrecting into vagueness would be a second
falsification in the opposite direction.

Output: seven corrected `.planning/` documents; four stale test counts brought to the
measured truth; and a cross-document mechanical gate that is the first contact this
provenance claim has ever had with a check.
</objective>
