<objective>
Harden the ledger reader into the refuse-by-name shape BUILD-05 criterion 4 requires, give
it its own test file, and pin the three edge classes the address join turns on — exact
adjacency, empty, and ordering.

Purpose: criterion 4's "with the ledger absent, the exporter declines by name rather than
inventing a verdict, observed" is a refusal contract, and a refusal contract is only worth
something when every way of being absent or malformed has a named message and a test that
observed it. The join edges are where a "provenance-aware" export would otherwise quietly
attach the wrong range's verdict to a block.
Output: the reader's full refusal set, a sibling test file, an information-disclosure
control, and the BUILD-05 adjacency/empty/ordering assertions.
</objective>
