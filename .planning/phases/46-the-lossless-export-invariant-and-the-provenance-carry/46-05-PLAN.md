<objective>
Make a user-requested exclusion emit as a recorded, visible, byte-complete block — the
half of BUILD-07 that turns "no silent hole" from a claim into a mechanism — and prove the
export can be read back to recover what was excluded and why.

Purpose: criterion 2's own words are "its identity and extent readable in the output,
marked as something the user asked for — never as a silent hole. Reading the export back
recovers what was excluded and why." The single most likely wrong implementation is the
obvious one: "exclude" reads, in isolation, like "do not emit". This plan makes the
opposite true by construction and asserts it against the byte arrays rather than the prose.
Output: exclusion-aware block emission, a readback test, the empty and ordering edges, and
an ACME round trip over an annotated export.
</objective>
