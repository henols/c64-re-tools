<objective>
Put the exclusion setter and its inverse on the `anno_*` MCP surface, so the user's request
can be recorded mid-session by the agent the user is talking to, and record WHY both verbs
exist in the one place this project keeps that judgement.

Purpose: BUILD-05's invariant is that an exclusion is "one the user asked for, made
explicit and recorded". The asking needs a route. Per assumption A4, an exclusion is exactly
the small bounded piece of state an MCP tool should carry — unlike the export's output tree,
which stays CLI-only.
Output: two tool definitions with their four registration sites, two register entries, tool
tests, and the skill documentation that makes the route discoverable.

**Site 1 — `ANNO_TOOL_DEFINITIONS`.** Add two entries immediately after the two scope

`anno_exclude_range` takes `...STORE_PROPERTY`, `start_address`, `end_address`, `reason`,

`["store", "start_address", "end_address", "reason"]`. Its `description` carries four things,
in the voice the scope entries use:
  - What it records: the user's request to leave a span out, WITH the reason, as a durable row.
  - The sentence that stops the verb being misread, stated first and plainly: recording an
    exclusion does not remove anything. The export still emits every byte of that span; the
    record is what makes the request visible in the output instead of invisible as a gap.
    What gets reversed, kept or left out is the end-user's decision, and this verb is how the
    user states it — it is not the tool deciding.
  - The refusal behaviour: an overlapping span is REFUSED naming both spans; two records that
    merely TOUCH at a boundary are disjoint and both accepted; an identical repeat SUCCEEDS
    reporting `changed: false`; the same extent with a DIFFERENT reason is REFUSED rather than
    overwriting the stored reason.
  - A MIND THE ENDS warning in the scope entries' own shape, naming a concrete transposed-end
    mistake and `anno_include_range` as the recovery route rather than burning revisions off
    the 32-deep snapshot ring.

`anno_include_range` takes `...STORE_PROPERTY`, `start_address`, `end_address`,
`...BASE_REVISION_PROPERTY`; `required` is `["store", "start_address", "end_address"]`. Its
`description` states it is the exact inverse; that both stored ends must match exactly
because a record is never trimmed, split or partially removed; that reading the stored spans
first is the way to be sure; and that removing a record that is not there SUCCEEDS reporting
