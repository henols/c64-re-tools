<objective>
Give the store a place to record "the user asked to leave `$XXXX..$YYYY` out, for this
reason" — the one piece of state BUILD-07 needs that does not exist anywhere today.

Purpose: BUILD-07's invariant is that an exclusion is a RECORDED, VISIBLE fact rather than
a silent hole. That requires somewhere to record it, and the record has to carry the reason
as well as the extent, because criterion 2 asks the export to recover "what was excluded
and why". The record is persistent, revisioned project state, so it belongs in the store
beside every other store-owned fact.
Output: `SCHEMA_VERSION` 5, the `anno_excluded_range` table with its index, three store
verbs, and the store-level tests including BUILD-07's adjacency edge.
</objective>
