<objective>
Build the two subject variants this phase measures against.

The first is a regressed twin. It is the committed hazard subject with three
single-bit regressions planted at the immediate operands feeding `$D020`,
`$D015` and `$D018`. It is the red control for ROADMAP criterion 1. Each planted
change is one bit on purpose. Under `compare.mjs`'s existing rules all three
would pass twice over, once as masked I/O and once as tolerated drift. Under the
narrowed mask from plan 50-01 all three must fail.

The second is the modified subject. One behaviour is removed and one is added.
Each is anchored to a named finding in the subject's own committed hazard
report. It is the material for ROADMAP criterion 4.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 4 requires that the demonstration touch decomposed
and rebuilt code. It must not touch an already-easy already-symbolised constant.
Anchoring both changes to named findings is what makes that true.

Output: three new ACME part files, two new `.prg` images and one new
annotation-store export. Two generators are amended. One test file asserts the
byte-level shape of both variants.
</objective>
