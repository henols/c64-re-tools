<objective>
Show the rebuild behaving like the original, and show the modified rebuild
behaving differently on purpose.

Two comparisons happen here, both by the mechanism plan 50-04 established and
plan 50-05 already saw fail. The first compares the original against a rebuild
produced from the committed annotation store. It must pass, and every remaining
difference must be named. The second compares the original against the modified
subject. It must pass under the pre-registered allowlist and fail without it, and
the two behaviour changes must be visible in the capture.

Byte-identity is recorded here as an optional extra and nothing more. ROADMAP
criterion 3 states that it is not the bar and is not claimed. The bar is
behavioural equivalence with a committed transcript as the artifact of record.

Purpose: ROADMAP criteria 3 and 4. The transcripts committed here are the
artifacts of record for both.

Output: a rebuild provenance record, two committed captures, the transcript's
green section, and a separate modifiability transcript.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| live emulator output → committed transcript | A value that nothing re-derives crosses into the evidence of record |
| the allowlist → a passing verdict | A document that can make a real difference stop failing crosses into the decision |
| a failing run → a passing run | A developer under time pressure can reach a pass by adjusting the instrument rather than the subject |
