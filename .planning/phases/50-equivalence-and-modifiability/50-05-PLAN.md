<objective>
Observe the comparison failing, before anything is asked to pass.

ROADMAP criterion 2 refuses a green-only result as evidence. An instrument that
has only ever returned PASS cannot distinguish a correct mask from a mask wide
enough to hide a real regression. This plan runs the deliberately-regressed twin
built in plan 50-02 through the exact procedure plan 50-04 established, and
commits the observed failure.

The three planted regressions are each a single bit at an immediate operand
feeding `$D020`, `$D015` or `$D018`. Under the rules `compare.mjs` carries today
all three would pass twice over. Under the narrowed mask all three must fail, and
each must be named.

Purpose: to make the later green result mean something. The red transcript is
committed first, in an earlier wave, so the order is a fact in git rather than a
claim in prose.

Output: one committed capture of the regressed twin and the transcript's red
section.
</objective>
