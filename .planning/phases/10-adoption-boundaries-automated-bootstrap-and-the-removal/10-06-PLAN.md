against a recursive-descent disassembler with an auto-analyzer.

`c64-program-recon` where static analysis actually belongs — both pointing at the single
seam, neither carrying its own copy. No seventh skill: one would cost the installer sync,
the plugin manifest and `check-skill-tool-coverage.mjs`, and would pre-build Phase 11's
home during Phase 10.

`disasm-opcodes.ts`, `disasm-decoder.ts`, `disasm-renderer.ts` and `stock-disassemble.ts`
are Phase 4's **protected** stock live-RAM disassembler family (ROADMAP § Standing
Constraints: "Phase 4's disassembler stays … all ~61KB of source and ~55KB of tests are
load-bearing"). They share nothing with `acme-build`'s `disasm` verb except the letters.
A diff touching any of them in this plan is a scope error, not a thorough cleanup.

`installer/skills/acme-build/*` needs **no** manual edit: it is gitignored and
regenerated from `.claude/skills/` by `installer/scripts/sync-skills.mjs`'s `prepack`
hook, so a SKILL.md edit has exactly one home.
