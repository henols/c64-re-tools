<objective>
Ship Family B on the stock backend: checkpoint add/list/delete/toggle, watchpoint
add, and condition setting — together with the two guards CONTEXT.md pulled
forward into this phase because the hazard exists the moment DIRECT-03 ships: the
D-10 condition registry with fail-closed cleanup, and the D-11 `stop:false` trace
guard.

Purpose: this is phase success criterion 2, and the two hazards behind it are
correctness-as-safety issues. A condition that cannot be read back and leaks if
re-set means an agent can believe a breakpoint is armed and conditioned when it is
armed and *unconditioned* across the whole address range. A non-stopping checkpoint
on a hot address emits one `CHECKPOINT_INFO` frame per hit synchronously from
inside the CPU loop over a blocking socket, which mutually deadlocks client and
emulator.

Output: `stock-checkpoints.ts` and `stock-checkpoints.test.ts`. No dispatch or
manifest edits — plans 03-12 and 03-13 own those.
