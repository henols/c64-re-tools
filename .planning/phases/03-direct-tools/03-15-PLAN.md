<objective>
Close two Phase 3 UAT gaps that both live in the test harness.

**MAJOR — `npm test` never terminates on a bare host.** `vice-proxy.test.ts:2830-2845`
calls `startStandInServer()` + `listenOn()` BEFORE its `try`, then asserts a
precondition (`hostPath()` actually translates) that throws outside a container.
`finally { server.close() }` never runs, the orphaned LISTEN socket keeps node's
event loop alive, and the runner hangs with no diagnostic. The sibling test at
2770-2775 has the same open-before-try shape. Root cause is verified (orphaned
LISTEN socket on fd 21, observed with `ss`) — do NOT re-diagnose.

**MINOR — structural test 1017 red since Phase 02.** `vice-proxy.ts:3275-3276`
holds two `vice-proxy:` template literals inside a ternary passed to
`console.error()`. The detector at `vice-proxy.test.ts:3734-3750` exempts only a
literal *immediately* preceded by `console.error(` within 40 chars, so it cannot
see through a multi-line ternary argument.

**Planner decision — the DETECTOR is wrong, not the source.** Those two literals
are stderr-only, exactly the case the detector's own header declares out of
scope; the invariant is "no *agent-visible* message uses the topology-naming
prefix". Renaming them to `vice:` would leave the file's 10 other `vice-proxy:`
stderr lines behind, splitting the process's stderr identity in two for no
benefit, while leaving the fragile 40-character heuristic in place to misfire on
the next multi-line refactor. So: widen the detector, and prove the widened
detector still catches a real violation.

Purpose: BACK-02's standing gate ("the existing suite passes unchanged at every
phase boundary") is meaningless while the suite cannot finish, and plan 03-17
cannot push to CI until `npm test` terminates — CI runs `npm test`, not the
`test:automated` subset.
</objective>
