<objective>
Ship the machine-control half of Family D: `vice_machine_reset`, `vice_autostart`,
`vice_disk_attach`, `vice_snapshot_save` and `vice_snapshot_load` — the five stock
tools that either restart the machine or hand VICE a filename **the host** opens.

Planner decision recorded here (CONTEXT.md's open item 3): stock's
`vice_disk_attach` **keeps the fork's `unit` argument shape** (D-03) and honours
unit **8** only. Units 9-11 are refused with text naming the exact protocol limit —
`AUTOSTART` (0xdd) has no drive-unit field at all, so there is no wire route to
units 9-11. Reason for a refusal over a silent retarget: D-03 explicitly rejects
turning a silent no-op into the answer, and an agent told "attached to unit 9" when
the image landed on unit 8 would then debug the wrong drive. Recorded in
`docs/stock-vice-parity.md` by plan 03-05 as part of the D-14 approximation.

Purpose: this is the emulator-side-path half of the phase, which is the mirror
image of Phase 4's DERIV-07 hazard — there, translating is the bug; here, *not*
translating is. D-17 puts both directions in one legible place.

Output: `stock-paths.ts`, `stock-machine.ts` and their tests. No dispatch or
manifest edits — plans 03-12 and 03-13 own those.
</objective>
