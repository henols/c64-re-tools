<objective>
Capture one real cracked release twice, through the route this phase built, and
compare the pair under the predicate this phase committed — producing
`GATE-01`'s one corpus-dependent input as a value, whatever that value turns out
to be.

Purpose: this is the measurement Phase 23 recorded `could-not-run`. The corpus is
already on disk (two gitignored `.d64` images with recorded digests),
`Drive8TrueEmulation` already reads 1 under the argv the broker emits, and
`AUTOSTART` (0xdd) is measured working on the real image — so `not-obtained` is
much less likely than the pre-mapped narrowing assumes, and `fail` is
correspondingly more likely. Either is a recorded input. Neither is a reason to
abstain.

Two riders ship with it. The transient allow-list is **derived on the real
release** by the committed method, which is the first time the cap of 64 meets a
real frame-exact stop — measured reference points are 0 differing addresses at a
frame-exact `READY`-prompt stop and 300 at a wall-clock autostarted stop, with
nothing measured in between. And the main-CPU memspace assertion is proven able to
**refuse**, by observation: a drive checkpoint hit sets `default_memspace`, no
binary-monitor command resets it, and with true drive emulation in the loop for an
autostarted release that is reachable rather than theoretical.

Output: three committed transcripts and, if the derivation succeeds, one committed
per-release allow-list artifact. No corpus byte, capture image or snapshot enters
git.

This is a **live** plan and it is alone in its wave. Stock's binary monitor serves
exactly one client, so no other live plan may run beside it.
</objective>
