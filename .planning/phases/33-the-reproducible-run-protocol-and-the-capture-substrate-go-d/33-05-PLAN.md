<objective>
Pin launch nondeterminism on the stock branch, add warp and headless as additive
launch flags, and re-ground the stale warp claim in the same commit as the table
generated from it.

Purpose: `RAMInitRandomChance` ships at **10** on this build — 0.1% of all RAM bits
randomly flipped at power-up — which is the dominant term in the divergence this
milestone exists to remove. Measured on this host: two cold boots without the
block differ at 59 of 4080 addresses over the untouched `$C000-$CFEF` window, and
at **0** with it. `AutostartDelayRandom` also ships at **1**, a fifth
nondeterminism source `REPRO-01`'s text does not name, worth up to 10 frames of
random delay *and* a change of which keyboard-buffer feed injects `RUN`.

Output: a stock argv that is deterministic by construction, a fork argv that is
byte-identical to what it has always been, and a docs table that no longer states
something factually wrong about VICE.

**Note on scope, superseding a ROADMAP claim:** the ROADMAP calls the whole-argv
`assert.deepEqual` assertions in `broker-launch.test.ts` "avoidable" via an
optional field. That is measured wrong. There are **five** stock whole-argv
assertions, not three, and the determinism block is *unconditional* on stock, so
all five change. Only the `profile` half is avoidable by optionality. The three
*ordering* assertions do survive additions, exactly as written.
</objective>
