<objective>
The MCP-side callers move onto the one route, and SEAM-03's "same answer from anywhere" is
proven. ghidra-run.ts and dxa-run.ts stop calling `runHostToolFromContainer()` and call
`runHostToolOverEndpoint()` from host-tool-endpoint.mts, an in-package import (SEAM-01). The
two-route branch in host-tool-client.ts is left intact and dormant, as 65-CONTEXT.md's "Explicitly
NOT in this phase" requires. Phase 66 deletes it; this plan removes its last live callers only.

D-08 reaches dxa-run.ts: its `outDir` goes, and its helper files move to a temporary directory.
D-07 reaches ghidra-run.ts: results are read from the returned local paths, and a requested
export is reported by its downloaded local path. D-01 reaches the opt-in live suites: when opted
in, each starts its own broker.

**Recorded note on the convergence metric.** host-tool-client.ts keeps its `containerpath.ts`
import, because the dormant branch still references it. The metric the ROADMAP predicts at 3 is
therefore expected to measure 5 again. Plan 65-09 measures and records it, per 64 D-18.

Purpose: every production caller of a host tool reaches the broker through the one module.
Output: two migrated callers, three live suites on their own broker, and the same-answer proof.
</objective>
