<plan_decisions>
**What is being corrected, and on whose authority.** `42-RESEARCH.md` re-read two complete
VICE source and NEWS trees present on this host — an unmodified upstream 3.8 checkout under
the user's downloads directory, and this project's own fork checkout whose version header
records 3.10 — and cited exact line numbers in both. The corrections below are that
re-check's findings. This plan does not re-do the research; it lands it, with the raw text
attached so the next reader does not have to trust a summary. A summarised citation is
precisely what produced the two wrong attributions being corrected here.

**What is NOT being corrected.** The underlying thesis is untouched and gets stronger: drift
across VICE releases is real, semantic rather than syntactic, and has hit exactly these
commands. Two of the three attributions move to different release numbers; the third is
confirmed word-for-word in both trees. And PARSE-04's operative requirement — probe each
command on its own rather than behind one assumed flag — survives the guard-sharing
correction intact, because it is still the only design that is correct under all three
shapes the source actually shows.

**Scope fence on CLAUDE.md.** Exactly one bullet is edited: the dependency constraint about
the CPU-history opcode. Do not touch the Architecture bullet that carries the interception
citations — a tree-wide guard pins those two citations by line number and content, and an
unrelated edit near them is how that guard goes red for the wrong reason.
</plan_decisions>
