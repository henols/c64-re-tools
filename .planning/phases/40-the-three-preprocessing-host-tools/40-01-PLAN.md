<objective>
Land the `.c64-re-tools/` consolidation — all writers, clean break — and close
`host-tool.mts`'s two "NOTHING throws" holes in the same pass, because the second
hole sits on the line immediately after the first writer this plan moves.

Purpose: `D-36`'s ordering constraint 1 is a locked owner decision — the path
migration lands **before anything writes new output**, so every writer added
later in this phase is correct from its first commit. `WR-03` (`D-26`) rides
along because `host-tool.mts:2067` (the oracle scratch root, a writer this plan
moves) and `host-tool.mts:2068` (the unguarded `mkdirSync`) are adjacent lines in
the same function; splitting them would mean two plans editing the same three
lines in two waves.

The consolidation stands on its own merits after the cartridge tool's removal
(`D-34`): it was chosen as a deliberate fold of the consolidate-paths todo, not
as a means to a cartridge output path, and its justification — six scattered
write locations, ~40 lines of ignore rules explaining them, and a consumer
needing one directory to delete — is untouched. It simply has no cartridge runs
subdirectory.

`PREP-04` — "a failure is reported as a failure" — is this plan's requirement
because `WR-03` hole 2 is precisely a failure that is NOT reported as a failure:
an unhandled rejection in the standalone `host-tool.mjs` process surfaces to the
caller as the opaque "host-tool.mjs produced no output on stdout", which is
indistinguishable from a hang.

Output: one `toolsDir()` in `repo-root.ts`, seven writers re-pointed, a
`.gitignore` that collapses ~40 lines to one stanza, regenerated
`resources/*.mjs`, and two never-throw holes closed with committed regression
tests.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
The `assumption-delta` detector fired `pluralization` on this phase (signals:
`alongside`, `also`). The real generalization it is pointing at is not the binary
resolution — it is that **`.d64` acquires a second reader**: the hand-written
`d64-parse.mjs` byte parser (plus its MCP-side duplicate `anno-d64.ts`) and now
`c1541` over the seam.

- **Noun now primary:** *the `.d64` route* — one route, owned by `c1541` over
  the `host_tool` seam.
- **Decision:** `promote`. `c1541` is promoted to the single primary `.d64`
  route; both hand-written readers are demoted to nothing and deleted (40-06).
  This is not re-litigation — `D-04` and `D-08` already decided it, with the
  three costs stated and accepted; this block records that the generalization was
  resolved by promotion rather than by adding alongside.
- **Rationale:** `c1541` supplies strictly richer inputs (per-**sector** BAM vs.
  per-track free counts; first T/S plus the raw entry bytes plus the
  next-directory pointer), so keeping the byte parser alongside would leave two
  sources of truth disagreeing about a provenance signal.
- **Invariant test (accepted, placed in 40-06):** a committed check that no
  module under `src/mcp/vice/` or `src/skills/` reads `.d64` bytes directly —
  it goes red the instant a future phase reintroduces a second parser.
</assumption_delta_decision>
