  valuable outcome — the phase's Notes say a defect found here IS the finding.

the preferred driving mechanism cannot be established (for example a nested `claude -p` session

which rung produced the evidence. Only if EVERY rung fails may the result be `fail`, and in that
case the failing thing named in the record must be the harness, stated as such, not the product.

A plan that can only record success is not a valid plan for this criterion. Do not adjust the
procedure mid-run to manufacture a pass: if the documented procedure does not work, that is the
finding.
