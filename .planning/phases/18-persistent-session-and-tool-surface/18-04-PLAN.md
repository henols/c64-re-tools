<objective>
Make a crashed or wedged session a recoverable, attributable event rather than a
hang (SESS-02), and stop the proxy's own exit from orphaning a live child
(D18-22/D18-23).

Purpose: the tracer holds a child and fails simply. This plan adds the three
behaviours that make holding it safe — know it died, restart transparently when
that is lossless, and refuse loudly when it is not — plus the bound that stops
D18-11's transparent restart from hiding a genuinely broken project or binary
behind what looks like slowness.

Output: exit tracking, transparent between-call respawn, loud mid-call failure,
a bounded restart budget with a new named error class, timeout-driven handle
discard, a synchronous teardown kill hook in `vice-proxy.ts`, and a measured
answer to the stdin-EOF orphan question recorded as evidence.
</objective>
