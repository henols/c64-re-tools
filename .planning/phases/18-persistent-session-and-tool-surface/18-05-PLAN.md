procedures actually need: add `r2000_read_region` so a routine can be read at a
range instead of exporting the whole program (SURF-01), and execute D-36 by

and D-32 has now been carried unexamined for two milestones. The composition
makes the live `handler.rs:1894` `u16` overflow **unreachable by construction**
rather than detected and routed around — the same idiom the `--vice` guard and
the kill-by-handle decision use.

Output: two new curated tools with named criteria, a documented range cap with a
named error, the D-32 refusal retired in favour of D-36, and every count pin and
prose mention moved to 19 with no drift left behind.
