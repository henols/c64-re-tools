dead end shows up after one commit rather than after the crash-handling, the
mutex, the settings wiring and the tool surface have all been built on top of
it.

Output: `r2000-session.ts` (single slot, lazy open, synchronous single-owner
guard, kill-by-handle); `openR2000Session()` in `r2000-mcp-client.ts` with
`withR2000Session()` re-implemented over it; `runR2000Tool()` rewired with its
call-then-save body untouched; `r2000-session.test.ts` with the end-to-end proof

Deliberately NOT in this plan: crash detection and restart (plan 18-04), the
serialisation mutex (plan 18-06), the teardown hook (plan 18-04), the tool
surface (plan 18-05). The tracer holds exactly one live child and does the
simplest correct thing on every failure — it never pretends to recover.

failure tracer-first ordering exists to prevent.
