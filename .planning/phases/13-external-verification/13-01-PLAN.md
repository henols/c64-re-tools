<objective>
Re-record the three capturable `VERIF-02` binmon wire fixtures from a real
`x64sc` binary, decode what the real bytes actually say, and reconcile every
consumer that currently depends on their synthetic provenance — in one plan, so
the tree is never red between the swap and the reconciliation.

Purpose: `display-get`, `event-interleaved` and `checkpoint-list` are the
project's own protocol ground truth for the stock backend, and all three were
synthesized from spec text on 2026-08-13 because no VICE binary was reachable.
Two committed tests currently *assert* they stay synthetic, and two more
hardcode request-id values that are artifacts of the synthetic numbering. This
plan makes the fixtures hardware evidence and makes every statement about them
true at the same time.

Output: three real `.bin`/`.json` pairs, a capture transcript answering the
folded todo's six numbered acceptance steps, a rewritten provenance narrative in
three places, and corrected request-id coupling in `stock-protocol.test.ts`.
</objective>
