`hasDispatchContext()` accepts "two stores into consecutive zero-page addresses" as proof of
dispatch, which is how EVERY 16-bit pointer on a 6502 is built — including the ones read through
with `lda ($fb),y`. Require the CONSUMER instead of the construction, and — the part that matters —
commit the control that reaches the gate's INTERIOR, so the next tightening cannot be verified the
same way this one was.

decomposition work runs under it**", and ROADMAP Phase 20 is instructed to run this instrument
throughout. A structural-completeness number that inflates on the single most ordinary 6502 idiom
cannot gate that sweep.

Output: a consumer-matched dispatch gate; a committed, generated interior control fixture and its
immediate twin; a report-level statement of the non-inflation property through the shipped
`buildCoverageReport()`; and a mechanical witness that proves the control is inside the predicate it
constrains rather than outside it.
