Make the verdict unavoidable for whoever plans Phase 24: name it in the ROADMAP entry they
read as their first act, point at it from STATE.md, and flip the traceability.

likeliest outcome — `degrade`, meaning proceed but narrowed — is precisely the case such a
guard cannot check. That makes these three edits the entire enforcement mechanism, which is
why this plan exists separately and why it runs on the main tree.

Output: the pointers, the amendments written beside rather than over, and the traceability.
</objective>
