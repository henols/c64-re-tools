## 1. Verdict

**Round 2's three blockers are all closed, and the finish-line flow now works.** Every claim below was
re-derived this round against source, git and live execution — not read out of a SUMMARY.

Round 2 said: *"A user who installs stock VICE from a package manager, installs the plugin, sets
`VICE_BACKEND=stock`, and follows `c64-ram-capture`'s only documented boot procedure gets `?DEVICE NOT
PRESENT ERROR`."* That sentence is no longer true. The same walkthrough, re-run against genuine
`/usr/bin/x64sc` (VICE 3.9) on a broker-launched instance, reached a full **65536-byte** RAM capture
with a matching sha256 and zero checkpoints left armed, and the developer read the evidence and
explicitly approved it.

**So why `gaps_found` and not `passed`?**

Because the phase that fixed all of it was never verified. Phase 8.2 has no `VERIFICATION.md`. It has
six plans, six SUMMARYs, a green tree and strong live evidence — and no verification gate was ever run
over its six success criteria as a set. Its validation contract is still sitting at `draft` /
`nyquist_compliant: false` with every task row `⬜ pending`. And `08-VERIFICATION.md`, the report for
the phase DIST-03 belongs to, still says the walkthrough **failed** — a sentence that was true on
2026-08-19 morning and false by that afternoon.

This is a different kind of `gaps_found` from round 2's, and the distinction is the whole point of this
report. Round 2 found a shipped product that did not work. Round 3 finds a product that works and a
close-out that has not been signed. **Three of the four gaps are files that disagree with a reality
this audit independently confirmed.** None of them requires a new phase; all of them are closed by
running the verification step Phase 8.2 skipped and reconciling four documents.

The pattern is also worth naming, because it has now recurred three times: **a phase's own
VALIDATION.md and STATE.md go stale at exactly the moment the phase completes.** Round 1 caught it on
Phase 8, round 2 caught it on Phase 8.1, round 3 catches it on Phase 8.2. Each was fixed by hand,
after the fact, by the *next* round of audit. That is a process gap, not three independent slips.
