milestone whose thesis is "the ledger must be derived and guarded" closes with a
neighbouring, hand-maintained ledger table carrying three rows that its own work
falsified. The Drive8Type row immediately above already shows the right shape
(`**Fixed** — Phase 8.2 plans 02-04 landed the fix...`).

## Assessment

This milestone did the thing it set out to do, and the evidence holds up to
being poked at rather than read.

Three things stand out. First, the sequencing worked as designed: GATE-01 was
built first, and every later phase ran under it — including one live instance
where the gate refused a write during Phase 12's own verification pass, for an
unrelated red guard. Second, four of six phases needed a second verification
round, and each gap was closed by real work; Phase 15's verifier even retracted
one of its own prior findings on direct re-comparison. Third, the milestone's
guards caught their own authors more than once — 15-01's parser widening found
150 findings where 119 were visible, and 17-REVIEW's CR-02 caught the audit
gate's own expected-guard list drifting behind the guards this milestone added.

The one blemish is the QUAL-01..03 rows: a hand-maintained ledger table,
explicitly outside the guard's scope, carrying three entries this milestone's
own work falsified. It is bookkeeping and it is safe-direction, but it is the
same species of silence the milestone exists to kill, sitting one heading away
from the ledger it fixed.
